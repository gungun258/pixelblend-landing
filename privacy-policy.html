Privacy Policy
How we collect & protect your data
◆ 30

Last updated: June 1, 2026

PixelBlend ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it when you use our AI background marketplace.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email and phone number. We also keep records of credit purchases, generations, refund requests, wishlist items and seller activity needed to run the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate your account, process credit transactions, review refunds and improve the marketplace. We may send service notifications and, if you opt in, occasional product updates or offers.

3. Your Images & Uploads

Photos you upload are used only to produce the generations you request and are stored in your private gallery. We do not use your personal photos to train models or share them with other users without your permission. Backgrounds you publish as a seller are visible to everyone on the marketplace.

4. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted providers for payment processing, cloud storage and AI image generation. These partners process data only as needed to deliver PixelBlend. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain account data while your account is active. If you close your account, we delete personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep records for legal or fraud-prevention reasons. We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

6. Contact Us

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@pixelblend.app or use the Contact Us page in the app.
