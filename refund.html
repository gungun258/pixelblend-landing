Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026. Align this with App Store / Play billing rules and your actual credit packs before going live.

PixelBlend offers a money-back path when you don’t like a paid result, as described on the site (“Don’t like your result? Get a refund.”).

When we refund

  • A paid generation or credit pack purchase where the output is clearly unusable (failed render, missing product, severe artifact) and you report it within 7 days.
  • Duplicate charges or a purchase you did not authorize, after we verify.

How to ask

Use in-app support or the contact path on the homepage. Include the order or credit ID, the generation, and a short note on what went wrong.

What we typically don’t refund

  • Free credits or promotional grants.
  • Results you already downloaded and used in ads or listings, unless the file itself was defective.
  • Change of mind after a successful, usable blend.
  • Purchases made through Apple or Google that those stores must process — we will point you to their refund flow when required.

Timing

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Bank timing varies. Credits may be reversed if the refund is for a credit pack.
