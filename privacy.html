Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026. Draft for product launch — confirm storage, vendors, and retention with your stack before publishing as final.

This policy describes how PixelBlend Technologies handles personal data when you use our site and app.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, and login identifiers.
  • Photos you upload (product and background) and generated results, so we can run the blend and show your library.
  • Usage data: device, app version, approximate location from IP, and feature events to keep the service reliable.
  • Payment data is processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.

How we use it

To create your account, generate blends, provide support, prevent fraud, and send service messages. We do not sell your photos as a public dataset. We do not use your private uploads to train public models unless we clearly ask and you opt in.

How long we keep photos

Working files stay while your account is active and as needed to show history and handle refunds. You can delete items in-app where that control exists. We may keep limited logs for security and legal requests.

Sharing

We use processors for hosting, analytics, and payments. Marketplace licensing may share what is required to complete a creator licence. We disclose data if required by law.

Your choices

You can access, correct, or delete account data via in-app settings or support, subject to law. You can opt out of non-essential marketing emails.

Contact

Privacy questions: use in-app support or the Download / contact section on the homepage.
