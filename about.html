About Us

PixelBlend Technologies

PixelBlend is an AI background blend tool for how brands actually create. Drop in any product shot, pick any scene, and get a studio-quality visual in seconds — no prompts, no retouching skills.

We built PixelBlend for founders, sellers, and creative teams who need catalog, ads, and social assets fast. Upload a packshot or a phone photo, choose a backdrop from your camera roll or the marketplace, and our model handles cutout, lighting, shadows, and reflections.

What we care about

  • Speed: a usable result in seconds, not a design sprint.
  • Control: your product, your scene — you stay on-brand.
  • Privacy: your photos are treated as private working files, not a public feed.
