About Us

PixelBlend Technologies · AI background blend for modern brands

Our story

We started PixelBlend because product visuals shouldn’t take a design sprint. Brands need catalog shots, ad creatives, and marketplace assets every day — and most teams still bounce between cutout tools, mockup apps, and expensive shoots.

What we’re building

PixelBlend is an AI background blend tool built for how brands actually create. Drop in any product shot, pick any scene, and get a polished visual in seconds.

Upload a packshot or a phone photo, choose a backdrop from your camera roll or our creator marketplace, and our model handles cutout, lighting, shadows, and reflections — automatically.

Two images in. One visual out.

That’s the whole workflow — simple enough for a founder on their phone, powerful enough for a catalog team shipping hundreds of SKUs.
